## Challenge themes (pick exactly one)

1. **BFSI: Intelligent Risk, Fraud & Financial Experiences.** Fraud and risk detection, process automation, customer interactions, personalised financial experiences across banking, financial services and insurance.
2. **Retail & Commerce: Intelligent Customer and Business Experiences.** Discovery, personalisation, conversational shopping, demand planning, inventory, customer insights, fraud.
3. **Manufacturing: Intelligent Operations & Industrial Efficiency.** Efficiency, quality, reliability, supply/demand, sustainability. Turn operational data into insights, recommendations or actions.
4. **Media, Content & Digital Experiences.** How content is created, discovered, personalised, understood or experienced across media, entertainment, publishing, education and communication.
5. **Future of Work & Enterprise Productivity.** Knowledge access, collaboration, decision-making, workflow execution.
6. **Sustainability & Social Impact.** Resource efficiency, accessibility, resilience, community support, sustainable decision-making.

The dashboard's challenges page may have more detailed problem statements than the public site. Check it while logged in.

## Hard technical requirements

- Use **Google AI models** (Gemini / Gemma family) **or** Google agentic platforms (Agent Platform, Antigravity, AI Studio).
- Build **primarily on Google Cloud**. Solutions built mainly on another cloud are disqualified.
- Deploy on **Cloud Run, GCP or Firebase** with a live URL.
- **Fresh code only.** Everything must be written during the hackathon window. Pre-existing projects are disqualified.
- All materials must be in **English**.

## Mandatory submission artifacts

1. **Live deployed URL** of a working, end-to-end prototype (Cloud Run / GCP / Firebase).
2. **Demo video under 3 minutes**, as a public YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive link.
3. **Public GitHub repository** with the source code (this repo must be public by submission time).
4. **Deck converted to PDF** covering:
   - the problem and who it helps (community or business impact),
   - the solution and its alignment with the chosen theme,
   - the solution architecture,
   - the business case, feasibility and scalability.
5. **Theme declaration**: name the theme you are submitting under.

## Judging rubric (same for all themes)

| Weight | Criterion | What judges look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **40%** | Technical Merit & GenAI Implementation | Meaningful GenAI use that directly solves the problem; functional, sound implementation; Google Cloud architectural depth; scalability beyond the prototype |
| **25%** | Problem Alignment & Impact | Clear problem–solution fit with the theme; measurable real-world value for users or stakeholders |
| **25%** | Innovation & Creativity | Fresh, differentiated approach; GenAI used creatively, not superficially |
| **10%** | User Experience & Solution Design | Intuitive, accessible UI; components including the GenAI parts work together smoothly |

## Prizes (USD 30,000 total)

Winner $10,000 · 1st runner-up $7,000 · 2nd runner-up $5,000 · Best Use of Google Cloud AI Tools $2,000 · Most Impactful Solution $2,000 · Social Choice Award $2,000 · Best UI/UX $2,000.

## Eligibility and team rules

- Teams of 2–4. Each person can be on only one team, and each team submits one solution under one theme.
- Every member must be **21+**, a **working professional, entrepreneur or startup founder**, and **physically based in and a legal resident of a JAPAC country**.
- **No students, full or part time.** One student member disqualifies the whole team, even after the finale. IDs and employment may be verified at any stage.
- Only 2 members can travel to the Singapore finale. Flights, accommodation and event-day meals are covered; visas are the team's responsibility.
- The organiser gets a 6-month right of first refusal on exclusive licensing of submitted materials.

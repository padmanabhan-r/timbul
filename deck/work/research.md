# Deck research, 14 Oct 2026 (sourced; [S] = sourced, "not found" = no source found)

## Hardware
- Swell paper A4: £66/100 (≈£0.66/sheet) HumanWare UK https://store.humanware.com/heu/a4-swell-touch-paper-100-sheets.html ; US$128.95/100 MaxiAids
- PIAF fuser US$1,799 (HumanWare US); Zy-Fuse from £510 (Living Made Easy)
- Index Everest-D V5 embosser US$4,695 / £3,550 ex VAT (RNIB shop)
- ~48 Indonesian SLBs own the same Norwegian Braillo 200 embosser, most no longer work (Kusuma et al., Sewagati 7(6), 2023, https://journal.its.ac.id/index.php/sewagati/article/view/696)
- Kemenristekdikti Rp 390m per ITS braille printer prototype, sent to SLBs in Jayapura, Ambon, Pangkal Pinang (ITS News 2017)
- No-hardware tactile graphics: collage with Wikki Stix, wire, foam stickers, bump dots (Perkins, https://www.perkins.org/resource/how-create-tactile-images-everyday-objects/)
- Spoken labels 88–89% vs braille 77% accuracy (Baker et al., ACM TACCESS 8, 2016, https://milnel2.github.io/files/TACCESS.pdf)

## Who pays
- Indonesia 2025: 2,396 SLBs, 166,242 students, 4,229 blind (tunanetra) (Kemendikdasmen Statistik SLB 2025/2026 via GoodStats)
- ~2,800 braille titles in Indonesian (Mitra Netra, Jurnal Teknodik 2016)
- BLBI "Abiyoso" Cimahi (Kemensos) national braille production centre
- UU 8/2016; PP 13/2020 requires government budget for reasonable accommodation for students with disabilities; Marrakesh Treaty ratified by Perpres 1/2020

## Time per figure
- 1–5 hours per image by hand; science textbooks up to 500 images = 200–1,000 hours per book (Ladner et al., ASSETS 2005, https://tactilegraphics.cs.washington.edu/pubs/assets05.pdf)
- Semi-automated: 6.3–10.2 min human time per figure over 2,300 figures (Jayant et al., ASSETS 2007)

## Audio + touch
- Giraud et al., Frontiers in Psychology 8:930 (2017), 24 blind students 14–19: recall 28.8% → 54.9% with interactive audio model vs raised-line map + braille legend (3D model)
- Brock et al., HCI 30(2) 2015: audio-tactile improved efficiency and satisfaction, 24 blind adults
- Landau et al., JVIB 2003: Talking Tactile Tablet, faster maths tests (small study)

## Global
- 1.4 million blind children worldwide (Arch Dis Child 2017, PMID 28465303)
- Braille literacy US <10% (NFB 2009)
